Our Mission

Built to make workforce management simpler.

We believe that great technology should work for your people, not the other way around. Our mission is to eliminate scheduling friction so you can focus on running your business.

From scheduling headaches to smarter operations.

ShiftFlow started when our founders realized managers were spending up to 20% of their work week just trying to put together a weekly schedule. Between sticky-note time-off requests, text message shift swaps, and constant overtime miscalculations, the system was broken.

We built ShiftFlow to centralize everything into one intuitive platform. Today, we help thousands of businesses across retail, healthcare, and hospitality run their operations seamlessly.

Our Core Values
