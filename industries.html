Scheduling built for every kind of workforce

Different industries have different rules. ShiftFlow adapts to your unique operational needs, compliance laws, and staffing structures.

Solutions tailored for your industry

Restaurants & Food Service

Challenge: Unpredictable rushes, tip tracking, and complex front/back of house rotations.

Solution: POS integration for demand forecasting, seamless shift trades, and role-based permissions.

Retail

Challenge: Seasonal hiring spikes, fluctuating foot traffic, and strict labor budgets.

Solution: Multi-location scheduling, labor vs. sales tracking, and mass shift unassignment features.

Healthcare

Challenge: Strict nurse-to-patient ratios, certification tracking, and 24/7 coverage.

Solution: Certification expiration alerts, strict ratio constraints, and fatigue management tools.

Hospitality

Challenge: Large cross-functional teams spanning housekeeping, front desk, and banquets.

Solution: Departmental sub-schedules, cross-training tags, and centralized communication.

Logistics & Warehousing

Challenge: High turnover, rigid shifts, and reliance on agency or temp workers.

Solution: Open shift claiming, overtime prevention warnings, and mass communication.

Professional Services

Challenge: Project-based billing, client-site dispatching, and varying pay rates.

Solution: Location-based scheduling, varying rate pay-rules, and mobile clock-in.
