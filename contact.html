Ask a Question

Can I try ShiftFlow before buying?

Yes! We offer a fully-featured 14-day free trial — no credit card required. You can start setting up schedules and inviting team members immediately after signing up.

How do I book a demo?

Just fill out the contact form above and select "Request a Demo" as your subject. A member of our team will reach out within 2 hours to schedule a personalized walkthrough.

How does pricing work?

Our pricing is based on a flat fee per location plus a small per-active-employee fee. We offer both monthly and annual billing, with significant discounts for annual commitments.

Do you offer implementation support?

Absolutely. Every new account receives dedicated onboarding support. Enterprise plans include a dedicated Customer Success Manager who guides your entire rollout.

Does ShiftFlow integrate with my existing tools?

Yes! We integrate with major POS systems (Toast, Square, Clover) and Payroll providers (ADP, Gusto, QuickBooks). Check our integrations page for the full list of 50+ integrations.

How fast is your customer support?

Our support team is available 24/7. Average first-response time is under 2 hours via email, and under 5 minutes via live chat during business hours.
